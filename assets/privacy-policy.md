# Privacy Policy

Quick Chat saves the last 20 phone numbers opened through the app and the time
they were opened in local storage on your device or browser. This history is not
sent to our servers and does not read your contacts or WhatsApp conversations.
Use **Clear all** under **Recent numbers** to remove it from local storage.

Opening a number sends it to WhatsApp to start a chat, subject to WhatsApp's own
privacy policy. The web app also uses Cloudflare Web Analytics for aggregate,
cookie-free usage statistics.
